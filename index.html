The stylist model

Not a subscription. A performance partnership.

Stylists grow by helping clients make better choices.

The founding stylist model is designed around referrals and completed work rather than charging stylists a fixed monthly amount just for being listed. Syllis can route relevant client demand to the stylist, and benefits can increase as the stylist completes more successful work.

5% Syllis service fee

For completed stylist bookings generated through Syllis, the platform takes a 5% service commission from the stylist booking value. This is a platform fee for facilitating the booking, not a government tax.

Questions worth answering

Before you apply.

Are the prices final?

No. Syllis is deliberately not publishing fixed subscription prices yet. The founding offers are described by discount or free-access period, and final prices will be shared before a paid commitment is required.

What happens after the 3 free months for users?

The premium experience continues according to the pricing Syllis establishes for the wider launch. Early members will see the terms before the free period ends.

How does the founding brand discount work?

Founding brands receive 75% off the normal brand rate for their first three months, then 50% off for the following nine months.

Why does Syllis charge stylists 5%?

The 5% is a platform service fee attached to completed bookings generated through Syllis. It covers the value of facilitating the client connection and booking workflow; it is not being presented as a tax.

Are all applications automatically accepted?

No. The early-access groups are intentionally limited and selective so the first version of the network remains useful and manageable.

Can suppliers have a different commercial setup?

Yes. Supplier and manufacturer relationships may need different commercial terms depending on the type of service, volume and relationship. Those details can be agreed during onboarding rather than forcing an unannounced public price.
